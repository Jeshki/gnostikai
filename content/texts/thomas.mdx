## Gospel of Thomas

A collection of 114 sayings attributed to the living Jesus, without narrative passion. The Coptic text is from Codex II; Greek fragments were already known from Oxyrhynchus. The translation here is Mark M. Mattison’s public-domain rendering.

_The 114 sayings are rendered by the reader from the public-domain Mattison translation._
